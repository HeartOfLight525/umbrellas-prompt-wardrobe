Outfit Prompt

The main dress is a teal and blue color.

Bodice & Upper Body

Neckline: Sweetheart/off-the-shoulder with a deep V plunge, framed by thin straps and a structured collar.

Structure: Heavily boned and fitted through the waist, creating an exaggerated hourglass shape.

Details:

Double row of large, round gold buttons running down the center front.

Gold piping and trim outlining the seams.

A wide gold cuff/bracelet on the right arm.

A thin gold choker necklace.

Skirt & Train

High slit: The skirt is split high on the left leg, revealing the leg and a gold-embroidered drawn on her legs.

Draping: The fabric is artfully gathered and ruched, creating soft, liquid folds that resemble flowing water or smoke.

Train: Extremely long and dramatic, pooling around the feet with more gold buttons cascading down the side.

Shoes: Tall gold stiletto heels with intricate detailing, matching the gold theme.
