Natural Language and Tag Style

Natural Language prompts are written in full sentences. They work best with Tsubaki 2 and Tsubaki 3.

Tag Style prompts are written as comma-separated keywords. They work with most models, though outfit details may vary by model.

How to Use an Outfit Prompt

  1. Copy the outfit prompt. Choose an outfit and select Copy Prompt.
  2. Add your character. Paste the prompt into your image generator. Add your character’s description, pose, or setting as you like.
  3. Match the format. Write your additions in full sentences for Natural Language or use comma-separated keywords for Tag Style.
